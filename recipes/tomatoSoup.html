Tomato SOUP!

Tomato soup is my girlfriend's favourite comfort dish.

Now that I have made it; I too fell in love. Check it!

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes I suppose. Make sure they're nice and fresh!
  • Onions completely make this dish.
  • Garlic
  • Basil
  • Broth
  • Salt & pepper
  • Cream!

Directions

  1. Roast those tomatoes, garlics, and onions! Wrap the garlic in tin foil. Cut tomatoes and onions into quarters.
    Make sure to toss all in olive oil, salt, pepper, then roast for 50 mins.
  2. Add everything to a pot with broth!
  3. Blend it up with an immersion blender optimally.
  4. Serve it! Make some croutons, crackers, grilled cheese, or just have it as is!
