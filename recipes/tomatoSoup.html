Tomato SOUP!

Tomato soup is my girlfriend's favourite comfort dish.

Now that I have made it; I too fell in love. Check it!

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes I suppose. Make sure they're nice and fresh!
  • Onions completely make this dish.
  • Fresh onion, garlic, and ginger.
  • Chicken broth, low sodium is prefered.
  • Canned tomatoes or roasted.
  • Chicken breasts
  • corn starch, but any thickener will do (for the sauce)
  • Heavy cream
  • Chopped cilantro if you don't have the soap gene. (sorry otherwise)

Directions

  1. Mix the spices.
  2. Saute the onions, garlic, and ginger in a skillet.
  3. Add the broth and the tomatoes, then let them simmer.
  4. Blend the mixture, then return to the skillet.
  5. Season the sauce and then add the chicken.
  6. Simmer the chicken in the sauce until it is cooked through.
  7. Add cornstarch slurry or other thickener at the last minute to thicken up the sauce.
  8. Serve over rice and if you're down with cilantro, this is when you'd add it.
