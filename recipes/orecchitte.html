oreccHITTE!!!

This amazing dish can be made in just one single pan!

A cheap, easy, and wonderfully flavourful dish that won't take up your whole evening.

Ingredients

  • 2 table spoons of Olive Oil
  • 1/2 of a diced onion
  • salt that suits your liking
  • vegan or meat sausages (make sure the skin/casing is removed)
  • 1/2 cup of chopped greens of your choice (arugula, micro-greens.etc)
  • 1/4 cup of grated parmigiano cheese (or more, we know you love parm)
  • 1 1/2 cups of chicken broth

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a deep skillet/pan/pot on medium heat.
  2. Add onion and salt, cook and stir until it has become clear. (approx 5 mins)
  3. Add sausage and cook for about 5 to 7 minutes, or until browned.
  4. Pour 1 1/2 cups of chicken broth into the mix and boil.
    Scrape the residue of the food from the bottom to reintroduce.
  5. Add your orecchiette pasta, cook in and stir in the hot broth.
  6. Add remaining broth when the liquid is absorbed into the cooked pasta. Most will absorb. (approx 15 mins)
  7. Stir your chosen greens into the mix, until it wilts.
  8. Add to a bowl, and sprinkle your parmigiano cheese on top!
